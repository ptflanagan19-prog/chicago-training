How benchmarks update paces

  • 5K result: Used to calculate VO2max-derived zones (5K, mile, threshold). A sub-19:30 shifts everything 10–15 sec/mi faster.
  • 10K result: Refines threshold and HM goal pace. A 43:30 10K suggests ~1:36 HM fitness — confirming sub-1:30 is very achievable with this training.
  • If both are entered, the 10K takes precedence for threshold and HM pace since it's a longer, more race-relevant effort.

The most important rule

Easy days must be genuinely easy — full sentences, never breathless. Most runners sabotage training by running easy days 30–45 sec/mi too fast. The easy pace range is wide on purpose. Err toward the slow end, especially in the first 6 weeks.

Strength Training
Two Sessions Per Week
Monday (Session A) and Thursday (Session B). 45–55 min each. Reduce load in recovery and taper weeks.

Session A · Monday — Power + Posterior

  • Back Squat (or Goblet) 3×8 @ 70%
  • Romanian Deadlift 3×10
  • Walking Lunges (weighted) 3×12/leg
  • Single-Leg Calf Raises 3×15/leg
  • Side-Lying Hip Abduction 2×20/leg
  • Dead Bug 3×8/side
  • Plank Variations 3×40 sec

Session B · Thursday — Stability + Core

  • Bulgarian Split Squat 3×8/leg
  • Single-Leg RDL 3×8/leg
  • Step-Up w/ Knee Drive 3×10/leg
  • Lateral Band Walk 2×15/dir
  • Standing Calf Raises 4×20
  • Copenhagen Plank 3×20 sec/side
  • Pallof Press 3×10/side

Periodization by phase

  • Weeks 1–4 (Base): 3×10–12 reps, moderate load. Form over weight.
  • Weeks 5–9 (Build): 4×6–8 reps, heavier load. Cut lower body to 2 sets in heaviest run weeks.
  • Weeks 10–13 (Race-Specific): 3×8, maintain. No heavy lower body within 48h of quality runs.
  • Weeks 14–16 (Taper): 2×6–8, lighter. Activation only — no new fatigue.

Key rules

  • Never heavy squat/deadlift the day before a track session. Shift Monday session if needed.
  • Calf raises every week without exception — most important injury prevention exercise for runners.
  • If very sore from lifting, back off load 10–15%. Soreness that bleeds into run days is too much.

Race morning protocol

  • Wake 3 hours before gun. Familiar carb meal — ~400–500 cal. Sip 16–20 oz water.
  • Arrive 45 min early. 10-min easy jog + 4×20 sec strides at HM goal pace.
  • Gel 15 min before start if using them. Second gel at mile 7. Practice this in training.
  • Nothing new on race day — shoes, socks, nutrition all tested in long runs beforehand.

Execution keys

  • Miles 1–2 will feel easy. That is correct — resist every urge to speed up.
  • Watch current mile pace, not overall average. React lap by lap.
  • Miles 9–10 are where sub-1:30 is won or lost. Every successful finish requires a strong 9th mile.
  • If 10 seconds ahead at mile 6.55, stay disciplined. Do not accelerate.

Taper reminders

  • Mileage drops ~40% Week 14, ~50% Week 15. Legs may feel flat — normal taper fatigue.
  • Do NOT drop intensity during taper. Volume drops, quality stays.
  • Sleep Thu–Sat before race is the single highest-leverage tool you have. Prioritize it.
